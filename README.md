## 🥘 Meal Tracker

A simple, browser-based meal tracking app that helps you log daily meal counts, calculate monthly costs, and visualize your eating habits. Built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, this app uses localStorage to save data privately on your device—no login required.

🔗 **Live Demo:** [Meal Tracker](https://abdurrahmancce.github.io/Meal-Tracker/)

🚀 Features 

- 📅 Monthly Grid Generator: Creates a calendar view for any selected month.

- 🍽️ Daily Meal Logging: Choose meal counts from 0 to 6 (in 0.5 steps).

- 💰 Cost Calculator: Set your meal rate and view total monthly cost.

📊 Interactive Charts:

- Daily meal bar chart

- Monthly meal totals (last 12 months)

- Monthly cost overview

- 💾 Local Storage: Saves all data privately in your browser—no login required.

🧹 Quick Actions:

- Save All: Store current month's data

- Clear Month: Reset entries for selected month

📖 How to Use:
- Visit the app: Meal Tracker

- Select your month and enter your meal rate.

- Click on each day to log meals.

- Press Save All to store your data.

- Scroll down to view your meal and cost charts.
